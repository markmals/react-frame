The pitch for MDX is that your writing can contain components. The trap is that your writing _will_ contain components, and in three years someone will have to delete one.

## Keep the vocabulary small

Our rule is that a component is allowed into the content directory when a third post wants it. Until then the second post copies the markup from the first.

Right now the list is four items long:

- `<Poll>` — a question with a few options.
- `<Figure>` — an image with a caption.
- `<Callout>` — a box with a tone.
- `<Terminal>` — a transcript that does not get syntax highlighting.

Four is not a target. It is what survived two years of people wanting a fifth.

## Frontmatter is a schema, not a convention

Validate it. Every field, every post, at build time:

```ts
let posts = defineCollection({
  loader: glob({ pattern: "**/*.mdx", base: "app/content/posts" }),
  schema: s.object({
    title: s.string(),
    description: s.string(),
    publishedOn: coerce.date(),
    author: reference("authors"),
    tags: s.array(s.string()),
    draft: s.optional(s.boolean()),
  }),
});
```

The build fails on a typo in a field name. That sounds annoying until the first time a post goes out with `publishDate` instead of `publishedOn` and sorts to 1970.

## Prefer data over markup

A post that says this:

```mdx
<Poll question="Do you test without JavaScript?" options={["Yes", "No"]} />
```

will still work after the poll is redesigned. A post that says this:

```mdx
<div className="poll poll--compact">
  <strong>Do you test without JavaScript?</strong>
  ...
</div>
```

will not, and nobody will notice until it looks broken on a phone.

The same applies to links. Write `[the routing post](/posts/the-case-for-nested-routes)` rather than hard-coding the production hostname: relative links survive a domain change and work in preview deploys.

## Drafts belong in the content, not in a branch

A `draft: true` flag that hides the post in production builds beats a long-lived branch every time. The draft is reviewable, linkable in development, and one line away from being published.
